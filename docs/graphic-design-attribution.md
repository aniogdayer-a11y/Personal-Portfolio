# Graphic design tools and student frames

The owner confirmed that the graphic design projects used Figma and AI for ideas, with the recycled booth as an exception. The booth lists **AI (ideation only)** without Figma: AI supported brainstorming and concept exploration, while the Creatives Team collaboratively designed and assembled the physical booth using recyclable materials. Other graphic projects retain **Figma** and **AI (ideation only)**. This information appears in the graphic gallery, each graphic project detail, and its artwork dialog. EduFocus's AI development credits remain separate.

The owner confirmed the title **BSIT & BSIT-BA Student Frames**, matching the text in the supplied artwork. Its eight designs are presented separately by program and year level, rather than as one reduced presentation board.

`ProjectArtwork` uses SVG viewports into the unchanged supplied image: JPEG for gallery previews and the original 5875 × 3189 PNG for the full-size viewer. No raster assets were generated, redesigned, or overwritten. The original source board remains available through the viewer's original-file link. Each frame region is approximately 1080 × 1080 source pixels. The gallery crossfades between the eight views, and the detail gallery and modal support individual inspection.

Each viewport also has an explicit SVG clip path, so neighboring artwork and the gray presentation-board canvas cannot appear in letterboxed layouts. The modal fits each artwork as a square, while original-size zoom remains available.
