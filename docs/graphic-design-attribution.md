# Graphic design tools and student frames

The owner confirmed that the graphic design projects used Figma and AI for ideas. Their tools are listed as **Figma** and **AI (ideation only)**. A process note explains that AI supported brainstorming and concept exploration and that the collection is not entirely AI-generated. This information appears in the graphic gallery, each graphic project detail, and its artwork dialog. EduFocus's AI development credits remain separate.

The owner confirmed the title **BSIT & BSIT-BA Student Frames**, matching the text in the supplied artwork. Its eight designs are presented separately by program and year level, rather than as one reduced presentation board.

`ProjectArtwork` uses SVG viewports into the unchanged supplied image: JPEG for gallery previews and the original 5875 × 3189 PNG for the full-size viewer. No raster assets were generated, redesigned, or overwritten. The original source board remains available through the viewer's original-file link. Each frame region is approximately 1080 × 1080 source pixels. The gallery crossfades between the eight views, and the detail gallery and modal support individual inspection.
